About CampusConnect • Adani University

CampusConnect is the dedicated student collaboration and campus ecosystem platform for Adani University, Ahmedabad located at Shantigram on SG Highway. Connecting undergraduate and postgraduate scholars across the Faculty of Science, Engineering & Technology (FSET) and Adani Institute of Digital Technology Management (AIDTM).

Students can build teams for hackathons, share engineering projects, find senior mentors, and discover curated Adani Group and Gujarat tech internships.
